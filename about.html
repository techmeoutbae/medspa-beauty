Founder story

Medical expertise paired with an elevated, hospitality-led client experience.

This portfolio concept is intentionally structured to feel like a believable premium business. That means founder credibility, care philosophy, and the client journey are surfaced early instead of hidden behind generic beauty copy.

Every recommendation begins with goals, timing, comfort, and realism.

After earning her medical degree at UCLA and completing dermatology residency training at Stanford, Dr. Chen pursued additional injectable and laser education in Paris and Seoul to refine a less-is-more treatment philosophy grounded in balance and skin health.

That same philosophy informs the Lumina client journey: consultations are paced carefully, outcomes are explained clearly, and every plan is built to enhance what is already beautiful rather than chasing trends or dramatic overcorrection.
